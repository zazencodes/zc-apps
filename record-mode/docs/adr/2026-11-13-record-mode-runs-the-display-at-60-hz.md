# Record Mode runs the display at 60 Hz

## Decision

Record Mode switches the display to 60 Hz. Normal mode stays at 120 Hz.

## Why

Screen recordings are 60 fps. At 120 Hz the Mac draws twice the frames the video can use, while it is also capturing and encoding the screen. Alex prefers how 120 Hz feels and accepted 60 Hz while recording for this reason on 2026-11-13.

## Rejected

- **120 Hz while recording.** Tried for a few days in November 2026. The pointer feels smoother to Alex while he records, and nothing in the video improves.
